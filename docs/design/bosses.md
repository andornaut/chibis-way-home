# Bosses

Every antagonist is lethal on contact with its drawn body, shows a half-second to 1.2-second tell
with a red silhouette outline before each charge or special, and pursues across chunks once it
shares Chibi's chunk or screen ([objects](../../gameplay/design.md#objects), [boss
behaviour](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-behaviour)). Bosses are an annoyance, not a fight: scored
decisions choose attacks and moves, every told attack has a named ordinary dodge, chases pause, and
each engagement gives up at a seeded 30 to 60 seconds. Pursuit also ends beyond one screen width or
at a room door, and the boss then keeps clear of Chibi until its rearm wait ends. Each encounter
stage holds one designed arena in the second half of its middle chunks, with at least two routes or
levels, a loop and a refuge that counters its boss, and no arena needs a skill ([boss encounter
principles](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-encounter-principles)).

Reference: [boss behaviour](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-behaviour), [boss encounter principles](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-encounter-principles).
