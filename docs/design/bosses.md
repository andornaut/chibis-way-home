# Bosses

Boss encounters use ordinary movement, route changes and refuges. Bosses pursue across chunks,
telegraph charges and specials with sprite movement and outlines, and remain lethal on contact.
Each encounter stage has one arena with at least two routes or levels, a loop and a countering
refuge.

Reference: [boss behaviour](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-behaviour), [boss encounter principles](../../gameplay/design.md#boss-encounter-principles).
