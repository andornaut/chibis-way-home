## Scope

Label the cumulative times Stage total, Zone total and Run total, and separate them from the
current-stage statistics with a divider on every statistics screen.

## Criteria

- The left column reads Last time, Best time and Attempts; the right reads the three totals.
- The full-page stage summary and Death card both show the divider.

## Decisions

- Last time and Best time stay as current-stage fields.
- Approval rests on the rulebook's Stage statistics presentation rule as committed in 44720d29.

## Outcome

The Stage statistics and interstitials and Death and retry sections define the layout and labels.
