## Scope

Build stages on 12 px tiles at a 720 by 360 base resolution, and tune Chibi's walk, jump, flutter,
coyote time, jump buffering and braking to fixed reaches in tiles.

## Criteria

- Chibi is 2 tiles wide and 3 tall; the screen is 60 columns by 30 rows.
- A short press rises 3 tiles, a full jump 6, and a running jump with flutter crosses a 17-tile gap.
- A ledge at a reach is always makeable, and one tile beyond it never is.

## Decisions

- Every jump has one uninterrupted decelerating ascent; a release blends toward a lower apex without
  a velocity snap.
- Neutral input brakes with its own acceleration.

## Outcome

The Grid and Chibi sections hold the reaches and jump rules; tuning lives in `src/data/bird.ts`.
