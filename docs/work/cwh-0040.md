## Scope

Make every antagonist lethal on body contact, with visible tells before each aggressive action, a
distinct special, awareness by shared chunk or viewport and pursuit across chunks.

## Criteria

- Contact follows the drawn body footprint of the current pose.
- Every charge or special has a tell of half a second to 1.2 seconds with an outline on the
  silhouette.
- Pursuit ends beyond one screen width, at a room door or by giving up.

## Decisions

- Each boss has one or two recognizable specials alongside ordinary pursuit and charges.

## Outcome

The Objects, Boss behaviour and per-boss encounter sections define contact, tells and pursuit.
