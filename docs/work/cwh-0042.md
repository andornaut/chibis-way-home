## Scope

Introduce each zone's antagonist once in a cinematic exchange, close each zone with an outgoing
exchange, give each antagonist spotting and escape barks, and schedule Fert's journey barks.

## Criteria

- The introduction plays once per zone per playthrough; play resumes as the actor departs.
- A bark shows only while its speaker is on screen, with no controls.
- Fert has two journey barks per zone, twelve in all, as an offscreen voice.

## Decisions

- Cinematic introductions are outside the encounter quota and have no collision.
- The separate foreshadow conversation was dropped.

## Outcome

The Dialogue, Quips and Fert sections define introductions, exchanges and barks; lines live in
`src/data/`.
