## Scope

Review the boss AI decision path and its use in arena qualification. Refactor a concrete
source of drift or ambiguity so the same observed state, rules and seed are easier to replay
and diagnose. Separate reproducibility from the criteria an arena must satisfy.

## Criteria

- Identify where decisions, seeded state, player forecasts and qualification results originate.
- Replace a concrete duplication or unclear decision boundary with a checked implementation.
- Preserve the recorded boss behaviour traces, game rules and seeded decision order.
- Keep arena targets and certification separate from diagnostic or repeatability evidence.

## Decisions

Behaviour and qualification policy changes require their own decisions.

## Outcome

The attack gate and scorer share the silhouette-gap calculation. The opt-in behaviour observer
records the actual scorer stream, sampled hold, input values, candidate scores and decision reason.
The runtime uses the same scorer and random draws.

Focused checks reproduced 418 recorded ability hashes, 16 entry-effect hashes and the existing
River Crossing and Familiar Neighbourhood still-encounter hashes. Arena qualification and its
existing targets remain tracked under cwh-0263.
