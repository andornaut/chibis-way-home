## Scope

A Tip speaker in the ordinary dialogue panel, with the name tab "Tip" and a 48 px light-bulb
portrait on the right. Tips are nonmodal and timed like the achievement notice: a tap dismisses
one, they queue behind unlock notices and play keeps running. Each tip shows once per
playthrough; the seen state is saved and confirmed Restart resets it.

Triggers:

- Bosses: the first time a boss acquires Chibi.
- Controls: the start of the first stage.
- Doors: the first room door in view.
- First power-up: the first grant.
- Red hazards: the first red lethal surface in view.
- Second power-up: the first time two are owned.

## Criteria

- Each tip shows once per playthrough, from its trigger, and confirmed Restart shows it again.
- Tips never pause play and queue behind unlock notices.
- Tip wording uses the inline Move, Jump and Use control icons.
- The save version is bumped for the saved seen state.

## Decisions

- The Tip speaker replaces the skill-glyph enable tip.

## Outcome

Implemented in 9c9078bd and dbd840b3: the Tip speaker, the six triggers, saved seen tips
(progress version 12) and the removal of the skill enable tip; Gameplay owns the final tip
strings.
