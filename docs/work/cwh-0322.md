## Scope

Raise the minimum to 25 distinct certified physics layouts per zone with matching ordinary
no-power-up required-route proofs. Prefer proving eligible existing layouts before authoring new
ones. Preserve existing certified pools until checked admission.

## Criteria

- Count distinct physical layouts, excluding arenas, cosmetic aliases and seed variants.
- Preserve existing route limits, hazard, slot and no-cross checks.
- Admit only final-tree evidence and preserve arena and approach qualification.

## Outcome

Apartment, Countryside, Home, Industrial and Woodland each have 25 distinct
ordinary-qualified layouts. Blue cityscape retains 35. The increase adds
ordinary proofs to 18 existing layouts and admits seven new layouts, two in
Home and five in Apartment. Existing arena and approach certificates are
unchanged.
