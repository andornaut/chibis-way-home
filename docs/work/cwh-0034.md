## Scope

Randomize every stage from a fixed start chunk, a seeded run of one-screen middle chunks inside the
zone's difficulty band and a fixed finish chunk.

## Criteria

- Chunks are 60 columns wide with matching seam heights and a clear seam surface.
- Difficulty grades run 1 to 5; each zone draws grades by its own shares inside its band.
- The seed never repeats a chunk twice in a row and avoids the zone's two latest stage entries while
  a layout fits.

## Decisions

- Difficulty rises in every zone after a demanding first-zone base.
- A successful ordinary clear targets about 60 seconds, or 90 on Apartment floors.

## Outcome

The Chunks and Stages sections define assembly, bands and lengths; recipes and shares live in
`src/data/`.
