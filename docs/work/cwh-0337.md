## Scope

Apply the shared Previous, Next and Skip rules to Opening and dialogue, including
the optional exchange with Mallow.

## Criteria

- Centre Previous and Next at the bottom with standard spacing. Previous is
  secondary and follows the visited path. Next is primary and completes a final
  linear page. Centre a lone action.
- Keep Skip at the top right, available only when both the current page and the
  immediate next page offer Next.
- Leave protected choices visible and actionable. Preserve input ownership,
  focus restoration, existing completion routes and save behaviour.
- Qualify the first, middle and final linear states and the existing choice
  fixture, including a page immediately before a choice.

## Verification

The focused checks covered linear actions, immediate-next eligibility and the required
choice fixture. Native checks covered the Opening action positions and both sides of the
Mallow exchange. The change deployed from `ffbdae7f` on 2026-11-27 after the full gate and CI passed.
