## Scope

Draw cards, controls, dialogue and labels in one HTML layer over the world canvas, leaving world
art on the canvas.

## Criteria

- The layer turns with the frame and hit tests natively.
- The UI stays hidden until its fonts and images decode.
- World backgrounds, map miniatures and actors remain on the canvas.

## Decisions

- Text sizes and wraps at its font role; boxes grow instead of text shrinking.

## Outcome

Restart (501e60d9), Settings (f922011e), Pause and the skill HUD (e204029e), dialogue family
(3e708016), map controls (3b466976), room text (4689a322), and touch controls and stage progress
(eda72f44).
