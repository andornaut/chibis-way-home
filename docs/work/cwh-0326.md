## Scope

Put the secondary Previous button beside text-only primary Next in reversible story sequences.
Place Skip at the top right, as in Ada.

## Criteria

- Previous appears only when a preceding page is available and replays it without completing
  the sequence or saving a new result.
- Preserve Next and Skip semantics, required choices, and input and modal guards.
- Verify the affected controls, keyboard focus, fitted layouts and unaffected pagers.
