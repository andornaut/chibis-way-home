## Scope

Let Use at a door open a full-screen room with the stage held, and give every stage a skill gift
room.

## Criteria

- A door plays its opening animation, then shows its room until Chibi leaves.
- Leaving returns Chibi to the door, shown closed.
- The stage doors offer seven grants each of Dash, Double jump and Speedy across the twenty-one
  stages.

## Decisions

- Every door sits in a visibly larger building or facade on the door's world plane.
- A gift is saved on entry, before any dialogue acknowledgement.

## Outcome

The Rooms section defines doors and gift rooms; assignments live in `src/data/zones.ts`.
