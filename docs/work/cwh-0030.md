## Scope

Define landscape play on a phone first, with a floating touch for movement, Use above Jump for the
right thumb, a move hint, keyboard keys and mouse movement holds.

## Criteria

- A touch away from a button sets the neutral point; a drag past a dead zone moves Chibi.
- The move hint and touch buttons show only on a coarse pointer, and Settings can hide each set.
- In portrait on a touch screen the game draws turned a quarter turn.

## Decisions

- Use opens an overlapping room door, the nearest by centre, before any skill action.
- Mouse buttons over the unclaimed playfield move Chibi; a press claimed by UI stays consumed until
  release.

## Outcome

The Screen and controls section states the controls; the touch button labels live in
`src/data/text.ts`.
