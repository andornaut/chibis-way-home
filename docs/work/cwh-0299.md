## Scope

Remove the cheat session. Cheats, cheated runs and runs started from a run link save, bank and
earn progress, unlocks, achievements and statistics like normal play. Cheats chosen on Start
apply to the next Play or Continue. Restart returns to a new save.

## Criteria

- No cheat holds, discards or restores the save; returning to Start keeps cheated progress.
- Cheated play earns, announces and stores achievements and statistics as usual.
- The Cheats card no longer says that cheats stop saving.
- Restart still returns to a new save.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25): Invincibility is a saved setting: a device preference kept apart from
  progress, off by default, lasting across Start, reloads and Restart until switched off.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25): Bump the save version for the new Unlock all zones flag and reset older
  saves.

## Outcome

Cheats no longer hold or restore the save. Unlock all power-ups grants each power-up as a pickup
does, keeping the selection; Unlock all zones saves a flag that opens every zone and stage.
Achievements and statistics from cheated play are earned and stored as usual. The save version
moved from 12 to 13, so older saves reset. The Cheats card drops its no-saving line.
