## Scope

Anchor the touch buttons to the screen corners and define Use, Jump and Pause press areas.

## Criteria

- Every control at least 8px inside the view.
- Press areas never overlap the move hint's reach.

## Decisions

- Press areas grow by a share of the shorter side; Use and Jump press within 100 of their centres.
