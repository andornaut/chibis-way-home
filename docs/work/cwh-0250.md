## Scope

No permanent skill gift selects its skill, the first included. Each grant queues its enable tip,
and the player selects a skill on the HUD.

## Criteria

- A first permanent gift leaves no skill selected and queues its enable tip.
- Later and duplicate grants keep the current selection.
- The rulebook and source guide describe grants as unselected.

## Decisions

- This reverses the first-gift auto-selection of cwh-0070.
