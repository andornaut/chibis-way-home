## Scope

Show a map of the six V1 zones, unlock stages in route order, open a paged stage chooser on Continue
and save completed stages and Chibi's map position.

## Criteria

- Clearing a stage unlocks the next built stage; completed stages stay replayable.
- Walking back off a stage's left edge leaves it uncleared and shows the map.
- Reopening the app shows the start screen, never the map or a zone.

## Decisions

- V1 is linear, with no forks or laps.
- Clearing a zone's final stage animates Chibi walking to the next zone, which waits for the
  player's selection.

## Outcome

The Map and Runs and attempts sections define navigation and saves.
