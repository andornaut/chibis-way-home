## Scope

When the first offline installation fails, show game-kit's loading failure with a Retry action.

## Criteria

- Retry restarts the installation.
- The loading presenter survives a synchronous Retry notification.

## Decisions

- The failure copy and presentation come from game-kit.

## Outcome

Failures connected to the shared Retry (82f93dd7), with the presenter preserved (f5e4221b).
