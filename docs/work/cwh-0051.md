## Scope

Add one obstacle per zone: Blue's crosswalk signal, Industrial's gantry crane ride, Countryside's
windmill sails, Woodland's ravine gusts, Home city's retracting awnings and Apartment's swinging
chandelier.

## Criteria

- Each fills a chunk's right half after a half where Chibi can watch a full cycle.
- Each has an ordinary no-skill route and resets on retry and area entry.
- Bosses never ride or perch on these obstacles.

## Decisions

- The crosswalk route waits for the observed start of Walk under a user-approved exception to the
  route budget.
- Second-choice obstacles stay recorded as proposals.

## Outcome

The Objects section defines all six obstacles, and their module pairs are certified.
