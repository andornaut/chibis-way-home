## Scope

Mark rides with no safe exit as certified forced transit, and hold Pebble's targeted swoops and drop
bursts during a protected leg.

## Criteria

- Only boarding from a listed bank starts a protected leg.
- Deferral ends at the first safe forward exit or refuge offered.
- Committed attacks, projectiles, body contact and red hazards stay lethal.

## Decisions

- Unknown ride state never certifies a forced window.

## Outcome

The Forced transit section defines boarding, deferral and exits.
