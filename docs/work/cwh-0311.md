## Scope

Adopt the published Game Kit release. The themed crash details text uses the kit's readable
pairing, and the entry disc's ring comes from the kit's ring inset, so the game's own crash
override and ring art are removed.

## Criteria

- The crash screen's details text is readable with the game's theme, with no game override.
- The entry disc draws its ring from the kit in every state, with unchanged disc and press geometry.
- Every visible change is approved from before and after captures.
