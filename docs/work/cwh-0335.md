## Scope

Make Chibi playable with only the arrow keys, Enter and Space. Apply the approved
desktop bindings across gameplay and the active interface, including nested menus,
gameplay choices and recovery.

## Criteria

- A, H and Left move left. D, L and Right move right. Space and left mouse jump.
  W, K and Up also jump. E, F, Enter and right mouse use the applicable action.
  Mouse position does not steer Chibi.
- Foreground controls support arrow, WASD and HJKL navigation. E, F, Enter and
  Space activate the focused available action. Mouse activation stays with its
  original eligible target through release.
- Preserve touch, native editing and links, Tab order, focus restoration, modal
  isolation, cancellations and safe actions. Suppress the context menu only for
  a right press the game handles.
- Verify the keyboard route through gameplay, nested menus and recovery on the
  checked integration.
