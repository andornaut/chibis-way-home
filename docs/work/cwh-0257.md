## Scope

The font generator stops copying the Liberation TTFs, and the two served TTFs leave public/art/fonts/liberation/. The source TTFs in art/fonts/, the WOFF2 faces and the licence stay.

## Criteria

- No TTF is served or precached.
- The art build check stays clean.

## Decisions

- No runtime file references the TTFs.
- The user released the inventory's compatibility TTF entry on 2026-11-18 under policy 820117b.
- `build.ts --check` compares file snapshots before and after the rebuild and does not prune `public/art/fonts/liberation/`, so a leftover served TTF does not fail it.

## Outcome

`fonts.ts build` copies only `OFL.txt` and writes the two WOFF2 faces; the served Liberation TTFs are removed.
