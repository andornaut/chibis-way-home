## Scope

Never select a skill on its grant, and queue a tip in the unlock notice panel telling the player to
tap or click the skill to enable it.

## Criteria

- A new grant queues the enable tip once play resumes, behind unlock notices.
- The tip ends early once its skill is selected.
- The unselected skill state mutes every owned icon.

## Decisions

- Pending tips are not saved, and confirmed Restart clears them.

## Outcome

Landed with the enable tip; the never-select clause was replaced by first-gift auto-selection
([cwh-0070](cwh-0070.md)), and the tip remains.
