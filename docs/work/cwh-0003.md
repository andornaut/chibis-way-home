## Scope

Precache the game page, its resources and its art in a service worker so the game plays offline.

## Criteria

- Each build has its own cache of `/play/`, its linked resources, atlas pages and art descriptions.
- A controlled document reads its pinned build.

## Decisions

- Offline play runs on game-kit's offline-play interface rather than a game-local worker.

## Outcome

Precaching service worker (9a86aac4), moved to game-kit's `startOfflinePlay` (21dab946) and its
0.15.0 interface (581356f6).
