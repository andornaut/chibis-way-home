## Scope

Qualify the minimum ordinary layout counts in play order: Blue cityscape 35, Industrial
outskirts 33, Countryside 31, Woodland 29, Home city 27 and Apartment building 25.

## Criteria

- Count only physically distinct layouts with matching ordinary no-powerup route proofs.
- Prefer eligible existing layouts. Add authored layouts when the existing pool is too small.
- Preserve existing limits, route, hazard and slot checks, arena and approach qualification,
  and legacy eligibility until checked admission.
