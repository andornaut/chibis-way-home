## Scope

Grant three skills through gift rooms, one selected at a time on the skill HUD, with developer
grants that follow the shared saves and cheats policy.

## Criteria

- Dash is a short committed horizontal burst with a cooldown and one air dash.
- Double jump adds one airborne jump at half the first jump's rise.
- Speedy raises running and airborne control speed by 20%.
- Skill icons keep fixed sizes across states.

## Decisions

- Ordinary required routes never need a skill.
- There is no life counter or extra-life mechanic.

## Outcome

The Chibi section defines abilities, skill states and developer grants; tuning lives in
`src/data/bird.ts` and `src/data/skills.ts`.
