## Scope

Keep the FPS counter switch in Settings, aligned at the top of the right column. Restore
the readout to its original bottom-right position.

## Criteria

- The switch is inline with the Settings rows and retains the same label and off default.
- The readout keeps its original parent, rotation, insets and layering.
- Saving, cheat sessions and the device preference retain their existing behaviour.
  Show tips again remains in Cheats.

## Decisions

Use the existing switch and prior overlay placement.

## Outcome

FPS counter is the first inline switch in the right Settings column. The readout uses
the original bottom-right placement, insets and rotation. Its source and stylesheet
match the previous overlay byte for byte.

Chromium pointer and touch checks verified the right column, the default off state and
persistence after reload. Show tips again remains in Cheats.
