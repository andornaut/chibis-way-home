## Scope

Match the shared Escape and keyboard switch behaviour in Chibi.

## Criteria

- A fresh Escape on the ready Start screen opens its Menu through the existing
  Menu action. During uncovered pausable play it opens Pause.
- Escape on foreground surfaces preserves their existing dismissal and guards.
  From Settings opened by Pause, Escape restores Pause and the next fresh press
  resumes play.
- Direction keys move focus. Enter and Space each toggle a focused available
  switch once. Preserve repeat protection and separate platform Back behaviour.
