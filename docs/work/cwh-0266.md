## Scope

Move Chibi onto game-kit 0.59's loader: a complete, verified install before play with
determinate progress, a silent switch to a staged update at launch, a game-drawn Update now
prompt wherever the player is that saves before switching, separate first-install and update
failure states, the kit's service worker entry and build manifest, and Exit through
`canExitApp`. Requalify the seven arenas once against the released 0.59.x URI.

## Criteria

- A first install downloads and verifies the whole build before play and then plays offline.
- A staged update switches silently at the next launch, or through Update now after a save.
- The arena certificates are requalified once on the released 0.59.x pin.

## Decisions

- The adoption branch pins the release candidate for game-kit's consumer proof; main pins only the
  released archive.
