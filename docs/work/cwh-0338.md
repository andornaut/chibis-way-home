## Scope

Measure representative active play on a pinned production baseline, then find and
qualify a change that improves the 99th-percentile frame interval.

## Criteria

- Fix the seeded moving Ravine route, trusted inputs and renderer settings before
  measuring. Use the current delivered runtime, with a fresh baseline after any
  intervening gameplay or rendering change.
- Retain raw frame timing, per-run nearest-rank p99 and variability across repeated
  counterbalanced before and after runs. Demonstrate a repeatable reduction of at
  least ten percent.
- Preserve gameplay, appearance, saves and qualification evidence. Ship through
  the existing release checks.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-27; answers chibi-performance-baseline-20261127): we won't measure on a phone. do what you can without

## Outcome

The user retired the shared 10% target. The shipped `ffbdae7f` Ravine baseline
measured 48.012 ms and 46.785 ms native p99 on SwiftShader. A separate Radeon
diagnostic stayed near the display refresh interval. Keep the baselines and
traces for a reproducible stall investigation. No rendering change shipped
under this item.
