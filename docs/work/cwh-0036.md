## Scope

Replace spikes and lethal objects with a common solid red edge strip on any side of terrain, with a
camera shake on actual red contact and physical red floors in valleys.

## Criteria

- The strip is 4 px thick and can cover the top, bottom, left or right edge.
- Only actual red hazard contact shakes the world; Reduced motion shows a steady border instead.
- Every production valley has a physical floor with a red lethal surface.

## Decisions

- Animals have no permanent red contact outlines.
- Water is decorative and never an implicit hazard.

## Outcome

The Stages section's Edge hazards and Valleys rules define red contact.
