## Scope

Draw distinct gift types and stages for each zone visit. Two stages in each three-stage
zone get one gift, and three Apartment floors get one gift.

## Criteria

- Retain the deterministic draw, ownership and selection through same-zone map visits,
  retries, replays and Continue. Start a fresh draw and clear skills on entry to another zone.
- Keep at most one selected skill, explicit selection, ordinary route qualification and
  existing grant and no-skill stats guards.
- Record journey gift collection separately for All gift rooms, preserve earned
  achievements and follow the approved save-version policy.
- Preserve the City Boundary approach and qualify zero-gift and one-gift stage assembly.

## Verification

The exact release passed route, save and assembly checks. The existing City approach
and cold encounter were retained, and three explicit gift variants were qualified
with ordinary controls. The change deployed from `ffbdae7f` on 2026-11-27 after the full gate and CI passed.
