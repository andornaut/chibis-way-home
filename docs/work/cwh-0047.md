## Scope

Implement the approved active obstacle targets: a ferry transfer crossing with a perpendicular gate,
a stationary warned vent and a crumbling support.

## Criteria

- A warned vent is lethal only while active, and its drawn phase matches its contact phase.
- A crumbling support cracks on Chibi's landing, disappears, then restores once clear.
- Each obstacle has an observation position, a refuge and an ordinary no-skill crossing.

## Decisions

- Only Chibi's landing cracks a support; bosses, movers and projectiles never trigger it.

## Outcome

The Objects section defines both obstacles; they are admitted to the ground challenge pool.
