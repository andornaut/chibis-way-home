## Scope

State the stage design principles and vary the middle pools with climbs, moving ledges, low
ceilings, red gates, orbital hazards and compact ferry crossings.

## Criteria

- Each challenge has a clear required action on the primary route.
- The primary route never has more than one screen of unchallenged flat travel.
- Horizontal crossings use 22-tile bank gaps that need platform assistance for an ordinary jump.

## Decisions

- Judge variety by required actions, not chunk IDs or skins.

## Outcome

The Stage design principles and Chunks sections define the principles and challenge variety.
