## Scope

A death by touching a boss, or a transient a boss emitted, records the reason `boss` instead of
`hazard`. The reason reaches traces, tests and simulator output only.

## Criteria

- Contact with a boss's body or with a transient it emitted records `boss`.
- Crush, fall and every other hazard contact keep their reasons.
- Saves and the death card are unchanged.

## Outcome

`stepZoneBodiesInternal` (`src/rules/zone.ts`) names the reason from the contact's source, and the
simulator counts `boss` deaths beside `crush`, `fall` and `hazard`. An orbiting hazard reports its
own index, not its host's, so one hosted by a boss would record `hazard`; no chunk, module or
arena hosts an orbit on a boss. The recorded encounter and stage traces, the six sample arena
baselines and the seven arena certificates changed with the reason.
