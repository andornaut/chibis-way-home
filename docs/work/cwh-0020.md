## Scope

Route scene and support controls through one shared hold binding: a tap acts on release, and a
hold acts once and suppresses the release click.

## Criteria

- Every discrete scene action and support control uses the binding.
- Browser-owned controls expose native action holds.
- Action registration is validated at construction.

## Decisions

- The game supplies the hold duration; the binding comes from game-kit.
- The default hold was 400 ms with explicit overrides.
