## Scope

Add 26 achievements in three tiers: boss escapes, zone clears, death-free third stages and zones,
all gift rooms and a no-skill journey.

## Criteria

- The Achievements card lists every entry, sorted by tier, with none hidden.
- An unlock shows a nonmodal notice in the dialogue panel for about 3 seconds.
- Achievements use a separate save that Restart preserves.

## Decisions

- An escape on the same physics step as a death or area change does not count.
- Approval rests on the user's 26-entry achievement scope, recorded in the rulebook in 171418b6.

## Outcome

The Achievements section defines the catalogue, card and unlock notice; wording lives in
`src/data/text.ts`.
