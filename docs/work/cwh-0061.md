## Scope

Add a close X to the Restart card, and lead the focus order with the primary button on the opening,
the update card and the celebration.

## Criteria

- Restart cancels on Cancel, the close X, back, Esc, P or an outside tap.
- Tab and the arrows on the opening move through Continue, then Skip.

## Decisions

- Confirmation is handled once; repeated input cannot repeat the reset.

## Outcome

The Start screen Restart rule and Opening story focus order state the change.
