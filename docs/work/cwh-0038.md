## Scope

On death, pan to frame Chibi, play the recoil, fall, squash and bounce, then hold a required retry
card with progress and statistics.

## Criteria

- The world, input and active time stay held through the pan and animation.
- Try again restarts the stage in the same run and seed; Back to title opens the title screen.
- Outside taps, back and Escape do not dismiss the card.

## Decisions

- The stage start is the only checkpoint, retries are unlimited and there is no permadeath.

## Outcome

The Death and retry section defines the sequence, card and active time.
