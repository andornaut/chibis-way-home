## Scope

Show a manually advanced storybook opening on new game and confirmed Restart, before the map.

## Criteria

- Four beats show the friendship, the argument, the parcel and Chibi waking far from home.
- Continue advances without an animation wait; Skip ends the opening.
- Completion or Skip marks the opening seen; an interrupted opening restarts at its first beat.

## Decisions

- The opening does not explain the fight or resolve the friendship.

## Outcome

The Opening story section defines playback and acknowledgement.
