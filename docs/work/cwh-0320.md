## Scope

Qualify at least 20 distinct certified physics layouts per zone using the ordinary no-power-up
profile. Prefer proving eligible existing layouts before authoring new ones. Preserve legacy
pool eligibility until checked admission.

## Criteria

- Count matching ordinary proofs by distinct physical layout, excluding arenas, cosmetic aliases
  and seed variants.
- Keep existing 600-tick route, hazard, slot and no-cross checks.
- Admit only final-tree evidence and keep existing arena and approach qualification.

## Outcome

Blue retains 35 ordinary-qualified layouts. Apartment, Countryside, Home, Industrial and
Woodland each have 20. Existing layouts gained ordinary proofs where eligible. Apartment
admits four previously excluded obstacle layouts and one new stepped-pier pair. A translated
variant remains eligible under its legacy certificate without counting toward the target.

The count compares geometry relative to each entry, including supports, collision, hazards
and moving objects. The existing arena and approach certificates are unchanged.
