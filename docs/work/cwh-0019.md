## Scope

Adopt game-kit 0.56.1's strict published modal isolation: only the top dialog takes input, and
covered chrome stays painted, dimmed and inactive.

## Criteria

- The covered HUD, Menu entry and skill controls stay visible, dimmed and inactive.
- Pause shortcuts respect physical modal ownership.
- Vertical menus keep their primary action at the bottom.

## Decisions

- Covered chrome is dimmed, not hidden.

(eccbf566); physical modal ownership (acdf3a5b); released isolation policies (b199be89);
