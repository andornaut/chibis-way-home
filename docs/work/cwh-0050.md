## Scope

Slow bosses down to an annoyance: scored attack and move decisions, fairness bounds with named
dodges, chase pauses, seeded give-ups, wandering, a walk home and keep-clear after every chase.

## Criteria

- Each boss starts a tell within 20 seconds and its special within 40 seconds of engaged time.
- Every told attack names at least one ordinary dodge that keeps Chibi alive.
- Each engagement gives up at a seeded 30 to 60 seconds.
- Bosses keep their destinations inside Chibi's screen.

## Decisions

- Grounded chase walking matches Chibi's walk so he cannot simply walk past.
- Boss behaviour has its own rulebook section (rules review, 2026-11-12).

## Outcome

The Boss behaviour section defines decisions, fairness and chase relief; values live in
`src/data/behaviours.ts`, `src/data/bossScorers.ts` and `src/data/bossFairness.ts`.
