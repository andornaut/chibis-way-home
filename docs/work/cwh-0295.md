## Scope

Use Map's existing Back to title action when a tap lands in the letterbox and
the map is the topmost eligible surface.

## Criteria

- Invoke the same action once and consume the tap before it reaches Start.
- Keep the existing readiness and modal guards. A nondismissible surface blocks
  the underlying map action.
- Keep the tap and drag rules and all in-viewport controls.
- Cover both horizontal and vertical letterboxing and the resulting focus.

## Decisions

Previous, Leave, Continue and platform Exit retain their existing input.
