## Scope

Hold each stage's one boss encounter in a designed arena chunk, placed in the second half of the
middle, and retire the yard escape points and encounter chunks.

## Criteria

- Each arena has at least two routes or levels, a loop of at least 20 tiles and a refuge that
  counters its boss.
- An arena is certified only when a skill-free evade controller passes 8 seeded runs.
- Retries keep the arena's position.

## Decisions

- Start with one arena per encounter stage; more variants wait for user feedback.
- Encounters sit only on each zone's third stage and on Residential floors and Penthouse.
- Approval rests on the user's one-arena-per-encounter-stage scope, recorded in the rulebook in
  6001520b.

## Outcome

The Boss encounter principles section defines arenas; the seven arenas live in `src/data/arenas/`.
