## Scope

Qualify a minimum of 35 physically distinct ordinary layouts per zone. The previous counts
were 35, 33, 31, 29, 27 and 25 in play order.

## Criteria

- Keep existing layouts and prefer eligible authored layouts.
- Fill deficits with additional layouts using the existing ordinary no-power-up route,
  hazard, slot and grade checks.
- Check final-tree proof provenance, preserve existing arena and approach certificates,
  and land proof-complete batches through the normal delivery process.
