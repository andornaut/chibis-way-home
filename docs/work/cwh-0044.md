## Scope

Make Apartment building six standalone floor stages, Lobby to Penthouse, each with a lower spawn, a
zigzag ascent and an automatic upper exit.

## Criteria

- Each floor is one continuous area with its own fixed caps and checkpoint.
- Reaching the upper stair or elevator exit triggers the floor transition.
- Penthouse shows Fert's outgoing exchange, statistics and the celebration.

## Decisions

- Fert has no physical presence in Lobby, Restaurant or Pool, is introduced in Spa and holds
  encounters on Residential floors and Penthouse.

## Outcome

The Apartment building section defines the floors; the approved floor pacing plan is recorded in the
rulebook.
