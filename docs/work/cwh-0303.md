## Scope

Recent-chunk avoidance removes only chunks a layout can do without. Chunks every layout of the
stage needs, such as the single connector for each seam step and the chunks that lead into an
arena's approach, stay in the pool. Only the outcome "no layout fits" falls back to the whole
pool, and the plan reports whether the avoid list was honoured.

## Criteria

- An outdoor stage entered again draws different optional middle chunks from its latest 2 entries
  where its pool allows.
- A plan with an empty avoid list is unchanged for every recipe and seed.
- Any assembly error other than "no layout fits" surfaces instead of falling back.

## Outcome

- `requiredChunks` (`src/rules/assemble.ts`) derives, on seam rows alone, the chunks every run of
  a flat recipe holds: the one chunk for a seam step every run takes, a sole arena approach
  predecessor and the chain that reaches it. It costs 0.01 to 0.07 ms per recipe and is not
  cached. `assembleZoneAvoiding` keeps those chunks in the narrowed pool.
- Only `NoAssemblyError`, or a narrowed pool that is no valid pool, falls back to the whole pools.
  Any other error is thrown on.
- `planStage` returns `avoidance`: `honoured` or `fallback`.
- Fallback rate over 8 chains per zone, each stage avoiding the zone's latest 2 entries:

  | Recipes                                 | Before, 1 or 2 entries | After, 1 entry | After, 2 entries |
  | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
  | blue, countryside, industrial, woodland | 100%                   | 0%             | 0%               |
  | home                                    | 100%                   | 0%             | 0 to 12%         |

- `src/rules/zonePlans.golden.json`: the `avoiding` hashes of the 15 outdoor recipes changed, the
  `avoiding` hashes of the 7 other recipes and every `whole` hash did not.
