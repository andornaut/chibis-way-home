## Scope

Recent-chunk avoidance removes only chunks a layout can do without. Chunks every layout of the
stage needs, such as the single connector for each seam step and the chunks that lead into an
arena's approach, stay in the pool. Only the outcome "no layout fits" falls back to the whole
pool, and the plan reports whether the avoid list was honoured.

## Criteria

- An outdoor stage entered again draws different optional middle chunks from its latest 2 entries
  where its pool allows.
- A plan with an empty avoid list is unchanged for every recipe and seed.
- Any assembly error other than "no layout fits" surfaces instead of falling back.
