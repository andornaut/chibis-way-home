## Scope

Capture one explicit stage, seed, camera and area from the 720x360 preview and export it as a 168x84
nearest-scaled PNG with a receipt. Tech owns the capture and Art owns the export.

## Criteria

- One invocation names the stage, seed, camera and area.
- The export is a 168x84 PNG scaled nearest from the 720x360 capture.
- Each export writes a receipt.
