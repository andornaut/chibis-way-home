## Scope

Give boss encounters one pure entry point. `stepEncounter` takes explicit context (tuning, object,
seeded streams, ground map), the behaviour state and the tick's input (time, target, world, door
and defeat signals) and returns the next state, its segments and one list of events. Door escapes,
defeats, spotted barks and keep clear become inputs or named stages of that step rather than
changes applied around it.

## Criteria

- Recorded behaviour traces, ability and effect hashes, boss simulator traces, arena certificates
  and evade hashes stay identical.
- The zone step reads boss outcomes from the returned events only.
- The encounter step reads no cache it was not handed and no value from the scene or the save.

## Outcome

Five of the six moves landed as specified and move 4 landed in an adjusted form.

- `stepEncounter` in `src/rules/encounter.ts` is the only entry point for a behaviour-driven
  object's tick. Its context carries the object, tuning, ground pursuit map, decision streams and
  observer; its input carries the position, step length, Chibi, the world and the unsaid spotted
  line; it returns the position, segments, state and one event list.
- Its stages are spot, hold, perceive, act, keep clear, stamp preparation and events.
- The zone reads boss outcomes from events only: barks, effects, escapes and the defeated record.
- Adjusted: boss death and door escape are not inputs of the same call. Both are decided from where
  every object's step ended (the death test reads the stepped position and the other hazards, the
  door test the stepped doors), so `settleEncounter` applies them to the stepped state and events
  after the steps. Passing them into the step would need a thunk or a one-tick delay that changes
  the recorded traces.
- Recorded behaviour traces, ability and effect hashes, boss simulator traces, arena certificates
  and evade hashes are unchanged, and no fixture or certificate was edited.

Remaining obstacles to full purity:

- The zone reads the pursuit map cache that `startZone` warms and hands the entry to the step.
- The traversal frame in the world still holds a `sample` thunk that re-steps other objects on
  demand, and timed hazard samplers call the act stage directly.
- `removedBoss` and behaviour activation change behaviour state outside the step, at area entry.
- The observer in the context records diagnostics as a side channel.
