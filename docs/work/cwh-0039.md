## Scope

Name the game Chibi's Way Home, the player bird Chibi, the ferret Fert and the zone antagonists, and
apply the names in player text.

## Criteria

- The cast table names Archie, Pebble, Trixi, Bini, Kiwi and Fert with their zones.
- Player strings use the confirmed names.

## Decisions

- Each antagonist has a distinct voice: Archie earnest, Pebble smug, Trixi rule-inventing, Bini a
  distracted gamer, Kiwi dry and Fert theatrical.

## Outcome

The Cast and Voice sections hold the names and voices.
