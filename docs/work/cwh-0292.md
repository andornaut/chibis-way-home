## Scope

Fit Chibi's Settings contents without horizontal or vertical scrolling. Match Ada's
switch and state-label structure in Settings and Cheats.

## Criteria

- The left column has the two touch-control switches, each with a short description.
- The right column starts with FPS counter, then Reduced motion and optional Install.
- The decorative FPS counter icon matches Ada's Settings glyph.
- The visible state sits centred below each switch in Settings and Cheats.
- All labels, controls and the centred Back action fit and remain usable.

## Decisions

Use Ada's frame-rate glyph and switch-stack order with Chibi's existing row and
modal controls.
