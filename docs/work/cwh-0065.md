## Scope

Add half-chunk modules and admit physically distinct pairs that pass an ordinary no-skill proof into
the Industrial, Countryside and Woodland pools.

## Criteria

- Each admitted pair fits its zone's grade band with passing pool, route and chunk-walk tests.
- Proofs use ordinary tuning within the 600-tick route budget.

## Decisions

- Equivalent geometry does not count as a new layout.

## Outcome

Industrial and Countryside gained ordinary entries, and Woodland reached 18 ordinary layouts.
