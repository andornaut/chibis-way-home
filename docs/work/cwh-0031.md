## Scope

Show conversations in a fixed box along the top, one paragraph at a time, with speaker name tabs and
side portraits, manual advance and required choices.

## Criteria

- A paragraph fits 2 lines of 408 px; a longer one is reworded.
- Chibi's portrait is on the left and other speakers' on the right.
- Continue and Skip are visible; exchanges never advance on a timer.
- A required choice shows its prompt and answers alone, and the answer ends the conversation.

## Decisions

- Every dialogue panel is translucent while its text, portraits and icons stay opaque.

## Outcome

The Dialogue and Voice sections define the box, choices and text lengths.
