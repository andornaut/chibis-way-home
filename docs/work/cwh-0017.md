## Scope

Show a two-pixel themed loading strip at the top for loads the player waits for, through
game-kit's progress adapters.

## Criteria

- Background loads show no strip.
- The strip stays painted and dimmed under modal windows.

## Decisions

- Loading follows game-kit's loader configuration and watch.

## Outcome

The kit's travelling and filling bar (be9b8de4), shown only for awaited loads (77926752), on
game-kit's loader (8c038337), painted and dimmed under modals (bddc47bd).
