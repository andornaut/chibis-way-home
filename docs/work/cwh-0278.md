## Scope

Use one arena slowdown rule across all six zones. Apply it to every boss arena, including
Familiar Neighbourhood. Keep the completed upper-landing trial as the cutoff for this
boss redesign round.

## Criteria

- An arena must take at least two seconds longer than its paired boss-free run in at least
  six of the existing eight runs.
- Every arena uses the same threshold. Keep the existing starts, ordinary skills, survival,
  route and loop requirements.
- The certification checks, evidence and rulebook use the same threshold.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-22): Use two seconds in six of eight runs across all zones. This replaces any local arena duration exception.
