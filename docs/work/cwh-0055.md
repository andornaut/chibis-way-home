## Scope

Keep Pause and the skill buttons visible through conversations, choices and departures, dim the
skill HUD where it takes no input, and hide the conversation panel under a required choice.

## Criteria

- Skill controls stay visible throughout dialogue.
- An inert skill HUD is drawn dimmed.
- A required choice hides the conversation panel, Continue and Skip.

## Decisions

- A skill-button tap advances only a conversation line.

## Outcome

The Skill controls and Dialogue choices rules state the HUD and panel behaviour.
