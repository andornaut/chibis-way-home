## Scope

Remove the impure spots left around the boss encounter step with identical behaviour: the pursuit
map cache read by the zone, the perception sampler, hazard samplers that bypass the entry point,
activation and boss removal at area entry, and the observer callback.

## Criteria

- Recorded behaviour traces, hashes and arena certificates stay identical.
- Each spot is an explicit value, dependency or transition of the encounter module.

## Outcome

Hazard samplers take the encounter step as a dependency, area entry and activation are explicit
encounter transitions, and observations return as data from the step. Pursuit maps are built at
zone start and read through one explicit function over a memo. The support sampler stays lazy as
a named dependency: sampling eagerly changed a boss simulator trace, so an order dependence
remains beneath it. The one value import cycle is gone, with its lint exemption.
