## Scope

Close the gaps the cwh-0307 Outcome lists between stage planning and boss encounters and the
shared procedural systems policy. Restructuring fixes keep output identical and land first. Named
per-attempt streams replace `seed + attempt` seeding as a separate, measured change.

## Criteria

- Stage planning returns a typed outcome with a reason when nothing fits, and each candidate
  refusal names its reason.
- Encounter entry and activation, timed hazard sampling and the pursuit map reach state only
  through the encounter entry points, with no observer side channel.
- The tell clearance has one definition, and no runtime module serves test fixtures only.
- No two seed and attempt pairs share a stream; the acceptance batch passes after the change.

## Outcome

Phase 1, the identical-output restructuring, closes every gap but named streams. Seeding is
unchanged: the zone still seeds `zone.seed + zone.attempt`, which phase 2 replaces.

| Gap                          | System    | New shape                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explicit results             | Stage     | `assembleStage` and `planStage` return `planned` or `nothing-fits` with its reason; callers with nothing to degrade to take `requirePlanned` |
| One gate per decision        | Stage     | `candidateRefusal` (`runSearch.ts`) admits a candidate chunk or names its refusal                                                            |
| One pure entry point         | Encounter | Area entry is an `enter` input to `stepEncounter`; activation is an `activated` outcome to `settleEncounter`                                 |
| One pure entry point         | Encounter | Timed hazard samplers advance through `stepEncounter`, handed in as the `EncounterSampler`                                                   |
| Reads only what it is handed | Encounter | `stepZone`, `stepZoneBodies` and `stepZoneObserved` take the prepared zone as their first argument; callers read it with `preparedZone`      |
| Explicit results             | Encounter | `stepZoneObserved` returns its observations beside the zone; no observer enters the step                                                     |
| One gate per decision        | Encounter | `withinTellClearance` (`attackGate.ts`) is the one tell clearance test of the gate, the chase pause and the pace pause                       |
| Runtime path only            | Encounter | `stepBehaviour` moved to `src/testing/behaviourStep.ts` and reads its step back from `stepEncounter`                                         |

- A temporary golden scaffold digested every stage recipe by seeds 0 to 3, afresh and avoiding, and
  every boss scenario's zone states plain and observed; its digests matched the base at every commit
  and it is removed.
- The certified lists' `verify` passed at every commit; `make acceptance` passes all seven arenas
  with the base's per-arena work and evidence.
- `assemble.ts` falls from 1776 to 1507 lines and `zone.ts` from 1838 to 1832. Explicit prepared
  zone arguments raise the size ceilings of `ZoneScene.ts`, `navigation.ts`, `simulate.ts`,
  `zoneWalk.ts`, `continuousRequiredRoute.ts` and `evade.ts` by one to five lines.
