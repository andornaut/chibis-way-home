## Scope

Pack every PNG the art manifest lists into atlas pages and load them in place of the separate
files.

## Criteria

- Each image is packed whole with its edge pixels repeated around it.
- Pages are lossless WebP with a content hash in the name.

## Decisions

- Packing and codecs come from art-kit's public Node APIs.

## Outcome

Content-hashed atlas pages (15d7a9b9) as lossless WebP (64500025), on art-kit's atlas and codec
APIs (43ada0de).
