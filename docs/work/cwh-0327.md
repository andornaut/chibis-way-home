## Scope

Reproduce the transient full-screen repaint on controls and fix the presentation boundary
that causes it.

## Criteria

- Keep each modal and its backdrop synchronized on opening and closing.
- Preserve localized press feedback, readiness, input ownership and focus restoration.
- Verify representative button presses and modal transitions in a fitted browser view.

## Outcome

Menu, Pause and conversation cards wait until their backdrop has rendered. Resume and Close
keep the menu card and its input scope until the canvas has rendered without the dim layer.
Existing button press feedback remains local to the control.
