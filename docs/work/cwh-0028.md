## Scope

Define the game's premise: Fert mails Chibi far from the apartment they shared, and Chibi travels
zone to zone across a map to get home. Set the world, the zones and the home page copy.

## Criteria

- The rulebook states the pitch, setting, core loop and story.
- Animals talk, there are no humans and there is no combat.
- The cause of the fight and the outcome of the friendship stay undecided.

## Decisions

- Chibi and Fert share an apartment in a big city; after a fight Fert packages the sleeping Chibi
  and mails him away.
- V1 follows one linear route, and completing it does not declare a V1 release.

## Outcome

The Pitch, Setting and Core loop sections describe the premise, and the home page describes the
mailed-away bird.
