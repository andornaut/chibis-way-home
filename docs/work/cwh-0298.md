## Scope

Move the touch Use button's centre to 70 logical px left and 70 px up from Jump's rest centre,
matching the shared offset in the other games. Placement only: press radii, the midpoint split,
Jump's corner reach, art and input wiring are unchanged.

## Criteria

- Use's centre is 553,213 on the 720x360 layout, moving with Jump.
- Touch on either side of the midpoint between the two centres presses only that button.
- The touch controls guide states the same offset.

## Outcome

Use sits at -70,-70 from Jump. A coarse-pointer browser check at 844x390 confirmed the placement
and the midpoint split. The intent tests probe Jump's radius on an arc clear of Use's press area.
