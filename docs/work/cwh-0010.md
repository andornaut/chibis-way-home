## Scope

A Dev link below the game opens collapsed stage, skill and invulnerability controls in regular
browser tabs.

## Criteria

- Installed apps and fullscreen contexts hide the panel.
- Loading a stage or setting invulnerability starts a development session, which skips save
  writes.
- Run-only cheats keep normal progress, saves, statistics and achievements.

## Decisions

- Persistent power-up grants are explicit and guarded.

## Outcome

Developer stage controls and contact protection (1b94ed36), grouped cheats with guarded grants
(78747fbe) and run-only cheats with normal progression (f1c48b02).
