## Scope

Verify the shared dismissal policy in Chibi's title Menu, in-game Pause, nonpausable Menu, direct
HUD dialogs and nested pages. Back returns to the invoking view, Close dismisses one dialog and
Resume returns to play. X, Escape and backdrop share the allowed one-layer destination.

## Criteria

- Leaving a nested page returns to the invoking Menu or Pause and preserves pause state.
- Close restores the immediate underlying view. Resume restores active play.
- Focus returns to the invoking control where available. Input isolation, mandatory choices,
  Cancel and confirmation guards continue to work.

## Outcome

Deployed in `d816ac34` after the scheduled gate and CI passed. The scene and input
tests cover title and nonpausable Menu, gameplay Pause and Resume, direct skill
details, and nested settings and achievements. They assert one-layer dismissal,
held-scene restoration, modal input isolation, and the existing required-choice
and confirmation behaviour.

Chromium checks restored the invoking Settings and Reset save controls and kept the
save unchanged on Cancel. On the exact final build, trusted touch on Achievements
Back, X or the backdrop dismissed the card and left its opener focused. These checks
used an isolated browser, with no physical device test.
