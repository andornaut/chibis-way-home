## Scope

Catalogue three stages in each of Blue cityscape, Industrial outskirts, Countryside, Woodland and
Home city, opening with a fixed parcel depot tutorial.

## Criteria

- Each zone has its own theme, palette and connected vertical routes inside a four-screen capacity.
- The tutorial's fixed start teaches depot jumps and stages Archie's introduction.
- No ordinary required route needs a skill.

## Decisions

- Stage one introduces the zone's antagonist, stage two has no boss and stage three holds the
  encounter.

## Outcome

The V1 route and per-zone sections list the stages and their layouts.
