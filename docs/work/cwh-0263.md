## Scope

A charge starts only with Chibi at the boss's height (less than 3 tiles above or below) and
between 2.5 tiles and the rush's reach, including a boss's opening charge. A charge whose rush has
no room to move, with the boss at its boundary or an edge and facing out, fails its gate: no tell,
no rush and no cooldown, and the boss keeps pursuing. It applies to every boss whose charge shares
this gate.

## Criteria

- No charge tell starts outside the reach and height band, including the opening charge.
- No charge tell starts when its rush cannot move; no cooldown follows.
- Recorded behaviour traces and arena certificates are refreshed after one bounded requalification.
