## Scope

Aim for at least 125 distinct certified physics layouts per zone, excluding cosmetic aliases, seeds
and boss arenas, while keeping ordinary no-skill route requirements.

## Criteria

- Only layouts with a matching ordinary proof count toward the target.
- Arenas are counted separately.

## Decisions

- Woodland was the first zone to take past the target, to at least 126 ordinary layouts.

## Outcome

Recorded in the gameplay targets, then replaced by a minimum of 16 per zone
([cwh-0072](cwh-0072.md)).
