## Scope

Remove the homepage navigation bar and footer, leaving the hero, About and
Controls in their current order.

## Criteria

- Play comes before Install in the DOM, on screen and in keyboard navigation
  when both actions are available.
- Play, Install and Controls continue to work at narrow and wide widths.
- Preserve the other pages and all in-game behaviour.

## Outcome

The live homepage at 320 and 1280 px has no navigation bar, footer or
horizontal overflow. Play precedes Install both visually and by keyboard.
Install opens its instructions, and the Play page responds successfully.

Released in `126c53a3`.
