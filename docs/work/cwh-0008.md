## Scope

On a touch screen in portrait, turn the game page's frame 90 degrees so the game plays in
landscape, and map touches through the turn.

## Criteria

- The frame turns only on a touch screen in portrait.
- Touches and Phaser pointers map through the turn.
- A zone pauses when the frame turns.

## Decisions

- The frame watcher and landscape stylesheet come from game-kit.

## Outcome

Turned frame (bb02eed2) on game-kit's frame watcher (0deee36b), with pointers mapped through the
turn (fcb229dd).
