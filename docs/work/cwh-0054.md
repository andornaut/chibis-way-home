## Scope

Give every control a screen-reader name from the player strings and keyboard focus rules, card by
card as screens move to the HTML UI.

## Criteria

- Focus stays inside the top modal card and returns to the opener on close.
- During play, the pause button and skill discs keep their names but are skipped by Tab.
- Conversations, barks and unlock notices are read out without taking focus.

## Decisions

- The map destinations and stage progress bar have accessible names.

## Outcome

The Interface section's Accessible rule lists every moved card; its CLEANUP sentence stays until
every screen has moved.
