## Scope

Qualify three distinct additive layouts with visible red undersides that can punish a second
jump. Gameplay owns authoring. Tech owns qualification and integration.

## Criteria

- Verify the ordinary route without a power-up and keep its original route targets.
- Show a supported full ordinary jump clearing the selected roof and a plausible fresh
  Double jump hitting its underside.
- Preserve existing physics, tuning, arena certificates and admitted layouts.

## Outcome

One new overhead-hazard layout is certified in each of Industrial outskirts, Countryside
and Apartment building. Each required route passes without a power-up. From the checked
approach supports, a full ordinary jump clears the roof and lands. A fresh second jump
near the apex hits the marked red underside.
