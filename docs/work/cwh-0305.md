## Scope

Give each outdoor seam step a second connector chunk, through five new authored shapes and reuse
of existing connectors across zones, and add two Home modules. The assembler keeps the least
recently used connector when every source of a seam step is avoided, and caps a stage at one
loop. Authored connectors get an ordinary 600-tick proof. One proving batch refreshes traces,
plan goldens, the six certified lists and City Boundary's approach.

## Criteria

- The first middle chunk of an outdoor stage varies between entries.
- Home's avoidance falls back less often than before.
- No stage contains more than one loop.
- Every authored connector passes an ordinary route proof within the 600-tick budget.

## Outcome

- Every outdoor seam step has two connectors. Five shapes are new: the drop descent, the guarded
  drop descent, the guarded terrace ascent, the gated walled return and the Industrial lift
  ascent. The others reuse existing connectors across zones. Outdoor recipes hold at most one loop.
- Over seeds 0 to 15, four entries running and each avoiding the latest two, no outdoor plan
  holds more than one loop and every honoured entry opens with a chunk unlike the entry before.
  One of Home's 192 plans falls back (`city-outskirts`); no other zone's does.
- Ordinary clears within the 600-tick budget: drop descents 341, the guarded terrace ascent 584,
  the outdoor terrace ascent 521, the walled return 403, the guarded return 496, the Industrial
  lift 192 then 362 and the outdoor lift 192 then 415.
- Home's list holds 24 entries, with the six ordinary-proved pairs of `homecity-garden-walls` and
  `homecity-crumbling-balcony`. The other lists keep their entries.
- A stage trace event samples every boss of its area beside the four nearest objects, so City
  Boundary's check reads Archie's attack at any distance. Its approach is qualified on the new
  seed 2 layout.
