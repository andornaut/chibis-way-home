## Scope

Settings shows Open Cheats beside Back in browser tabs only, checked when it opens and at each
action; it replaces the Dev toggle and the Developer controls panel. The Cheats modal is in the
large width family, its body scrolls and Close is the bottom primary button.

- Run-only: Invulnerability (contact hazards only), a per-power-up run enable, Load stage, and an
  FPS counter, off by default, in the bottom-right corner of the device viewport inside the safe area, drawn above everything including letterbox bars.
- Save-changing: Unlock all zones (every zone and stage available, nothing marked cleared), Unlock
  all power-ups, Show tips again (clears every seen tip), and last, after a confirmation, Reset
  save (progress, statistics, achievements, seen tips and grants; device settings stay).

## Criteria

- Cheats are reachable only from Settings in a browser tab.
- No cheat changes achievements or statistics except Reset save.
- Reset save asks for confirmation and keeps device settings.

## Decisions

- The shared policy is game-coordinator `docs/game-policies.md#saves-and-cheats`; the FPS counter sits bottom-right of the device viewport, above everything (user, 2026-11-18, policies 786f5b3 and 44743a3).
