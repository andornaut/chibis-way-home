## Scope

Release Tech-owned worktrees and branches already on main after their cited proof files are promoted to the protocol artifact store, and delete unlanded Tech branches whose GameKit or loading work a newer pin replaced after their diffs are saved as artifacts. Unlanded work that no pin replaced, dirty worktrees, protected resources and other roles' items stay; other roles receive their lists.

## Criteria

- Every released item is on main or superseded by a newer pin, with its cited evidence or diff saved first.
- Kept items are listed with their reason.

## Decisions

- Woodland and Blue candidate branches are not pin-superseded and stay.

## Outcome

Released 89 landed Tech worktrees and branches and deleted 13 pin-superseded branches after saving their patches as artifacts; 1,235 evidence files were promoted first. Blue, Woodland and Industrial candidates, the stage preview capture, dirty and protected items stayed.
