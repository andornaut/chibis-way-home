## Scope

Keep every control inside the game's 2:1 view, mirror Pause and the skill discs at the top corners,
and place Jump and the move hint from the screen's edges inside the safe insets.

## Criteria

- Jump's ring sits `CONTROL_SIDE` in from the right and `CONTROL_BOTTOM` up from the bottom.
- Each ring stays at least `CONTROL_VIEW_MARGIN` inside the view.
- Both sides use the larger safe inset so they stay symmetric.

## Decisions

- `CONTROL_SIDE` is 60 and `CONTROL_BOTTOM` is 40.

## Outcome

The Touch buttons and Move hint rules define placement; values live in `src/data/layout.ts`.
