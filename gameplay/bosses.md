# Bosses

Rules for noncombat antagonist encounters, from awareness and pursuit to tells, evasion and recovery. See [stage encounter placement](stages.md#stages) and the [playtest criteria](playtesting.md#boss-playtest-criteria). The [animal contact rules](stages.md#objects) apply throughout each encounter.

## Boss behaviour

- **Attack choice:** a charge needs Chibi at the boss's height (less than 3 tiles above or below) and between 2.5 tiles and the rush's reach, so high ground denies charges. Archie's pounce reaches 1 to 8 tiles out at any height its hop meets. Fert's parcel needs Chibi more than 2 tiles out (full weight from 5) and at most 15 tiles centre to centre, and is half as likely without sight. Trixi's trap needs Chibi on her own floor (less than 5 tiles of height gap) and not touching. Kiwi's drop needs her lined up under Chibi with a clear climb and Chibi above her (his feet at or above the top of her body), so she never climbs from beside him on the same floor. Bini's growl needs Chibi within 4 tiles of his height and is half as likely without sight; his maul needs Chibi within 5 tiles at his height. Pebble's drop burst follows the drop approach and his swoop follows the swoop setup with a 12-tile gap. These distance and height limits gate every scored choice of a charge, parcel or growl; a wander's opening attack on spotting Chibi is not held to them. A rush and its slide travel the way the boss faces, toward Chibi on either side, up to the rush's reach. A charge also needs its rush to have room to move from where the boss stands; a charge without room starts no tell, rush or cooldown, and the boss keeps pursuing
- **Attack sequencing:** each boss commits to one attack sequence at a time, retaining its identity through the required physical returns and completion boundary. The shared post-completion cooldown begins only after that sequence finishes; during it the boss can move and pursue rather than waiting in place. An aborted tell before commitment does not start this cooldown. Its duration is `ATTACK_COOLDOWN_SECONDS` in `src/data/behaviours.ts` (5 seconds). Every tell leads to its attack or cancels; there are no feints. After a second consecutive cancelled tell, the boss pursues for 1.5 seconds without starting a new tell; this pause is not attack cooldown. The consecutive count resets when that pause ends, when an attack commits or when the chase ends in escape
- **Attack tells:** every aggressive charge or special action has a visible pre-action tell; the action begins only after the tell. Shared tells last between half a second and 1.2 seconds: a charge 0.9 seconds, a special 1 second and Bini's growl 1.2 seconds, in shared behaviour data; Kiwi's re-aimed second-charge tell is 0.6 seconds. From tell start to the earliest possible contact (the tell plus the smallest gap its gate allows, closed at the attack's fastest committed speed) is at least `minReactionSeconds` (0.6 seconds) in `src/data/bossFairness.ts`. This includes swoops, drops, growls, pounces, throws and trap placement. A trap-placement tell is separate from the trap's warning and arming delay after placement. All six bosses play their small-hop or wing-lift anticipation pose once, then hold its final brace for the remainder of the tell. This is sprite animation, not a physical jump or a contact grace period. The renderer and lethal silhouette contact use the same authoritative animation clock and current frame. Only during an active attack tell, an outward red outline with an outer ink rim follows the current sprite frame's actual alpha silhouette. The outline adds no circle, clearance gap or pulse and does not enlarge lethal contact. Outline widths and colours live in `src/data/bossWarning.ts`. Body contact stays lethal during tells and recovery
- **Boss death:** a boss that touches a red hazard or falls out of the level dies, as Chibi does. It plays a short removal puff (held on its first frame with Reduced motion on), loses collision and ends its pursuit without an escape bark. It does not return during that attempt. A boss death neither counts as an escape nor prevents a later one. A retry restores the encounter, boss included
- **Chase pauses:** after a seeded 5 to 8 seconds of engaged chase outside attacks, the boss pauses for a seeded 0.8 to 1.4 seconds facing Chibi in its idle pose; Pebble holds a hover. A pause starts only with a movement-map path to Chibi, on the ground (Pebble in a safe hover) and at least 1 second after a tell or committed attack, and no tell starts within 1 second after it ends. Tells and committed attacks never pause. Values are `CHASE_RELIEF` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`
- **Enemy awareness:** all six pursuing bosses acquire Chibi when their current horizontal centres lie inside the same authored chunk in the current area, or when both physical body centres are inside the actual current viewport. Neither alternative requires line of sight or ground reach. Missing chunk metadata disables only the shared-chunk alternative; valid viewport awareness still applies. Rearming uses the same awareness alternatives with its existing settled and reachable guards. Attack-specific sight and exposure checks remain separate, and spotting barks still require an actual on-screen speaker at presentation
- **Enemy pursuit:** an acquired antagonist can chase across chunks in the same area, within six screen widths horizontally from its original encounter position for route planning. Entering a room door also ends pursuit as an escape, without an escape bark. Brief loss of sight, a jump or temporary unreachability does not end pursuit. Pursuit ends when the current two-dimensional centre-to-centre distance from the boss to Chibi exceeds one screen width (60 tiles), producing an escape bark. Within 60 tiles, pursuit ends only at a room door or by giving up. When no movement-map path reaches Chibi, a ground boss walks to the reachable spot nearest him, pauses facing him, then paces 2 tiles each side of that spot at 0.35 times Chibi's walk, pausing facing him for a seeded 0.8 to 1.5 seconds at every turn (`BOSS_PACE` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`); no pace pause starts within 1 second after a tell or committed attack. It re-checks the path at every decision and resumes the chase when one exists. After 6 seconds with no path (`unreachableGraceSeconds`), the boss gives up; the clock resets when a path returns. No boss waits in frustration or cancels a tell because Chibi is out of reach: every tell runs to its own commit or cancel rule. After every ended chase (give-up, 60-tile distance or door), the boss completes its committed physical recovery without starting another attack. A ground boss then walks at chase speed toward its encounter position, subject to Keep clear, under ordinary gravity, falling when unsupported and stopping at walls, support edges and the edge of Chibi's screen, and resumes normal behaviour where it stops, without teleporting. Ground bosses physically walk and jump across reachable ledges, blocks and gaps, following the fastest route on the area movement map for their body and movement, with each leg proved by the physical planner and safe landings rather than teleporting through cover. A leg whose physical proof fails is dropped from the route and the boss replans on the next tick without holding still. When Chibi stands on a moving support or the boss is off its movement map, pursuit plans the route directly with the physical planner instead. A committed ground rush that reaches a ledge stops at the edge and recovers there; it never carries level over a gap or past the area edge and does not fall off. A supported jump route or a committed safe jump in flight counts as reachable; flying pursuit follows its target within physical clearance. Grounded chase walking is Chibi's walk, Bini 0.95 times (`GROUND_PURSUIT_SPEED` and `BEAR_PURSUIT_SPEED`), so a boss keeps pace and Chibi cannot simply walk past and leave it behind. Pebble's ordinary flight (landings and returns to support) is 1.2 times (`PIGEON_FLIGHT_SPEED`); his overhead attack approach, drop alignment and relocation legs are 1.6 times (`PIGEON_APPROACH_SPEED`). Grounded chase walking uses the chase speed, while takeoffs, air legs and movement-map routes use a separate traversal speed, so jump reach does not depend on chase speed; closer attack engagement remains separately tuned. After escape, Pebble flies to nearby actual supported ground or a perch before resuming normal behaviour near his stopping position. If no safe reachable landing exists, he stays escaped with recovery pending, without attacking or teleporting. Charges, pounces, throws, traps, growls, vertical drops, swoops and physical perch returns use their authored local bounds, headings, speeds and timing from shared behaviour data. Every ended chase waits 5 seconds (`rearmSeconds`) before awareness can engage again; then connected-ground rearming applies when Chibi is inside six screen widths of the encounter position; other routes require a movement-map path from the boss to Chibi, on the same map pursuit follows. Rearming may start a tell on the same tick. Shared-chunk or current-viewport awareness applies to rearming; attack-specific current-screen and line-of-sight checks remain intact. Rearming checks run on every settled update while Chibi is inside the boundary and the boss is aware. No other timer delays rearming. No movement-map path is the unreachable verdict; a leg proof that fails or lacks geometry is not, and only removes that leg from the route
- **Giving up:** each engagement draws a seeded give-up time between 30 and 60 seconds. The first chase pause that starts at or after it ends the chase, and a pause still running when it passes ends the chase at once; a committed attack finishes first. Pebble gives up too. A give-up ends pursuit like the 60-tile escape, with the escape bark and the escape event, so it counts toward "Shook off {boss}"
- **Ground traversal preparation:** a verified grounded pursuit takeoff has a visible warning, with its tunable half-to-one-second duration owned by `GROUND_TRAVERSAL.prepSeconds` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`. A takeoff that starts within `GROUND_TRAVERSAL.prepIntervalSeconds` of the last preparation's start launches without a new preparation. The same once-played anticipation pose starts with actual preparation, including idle-recovery takeoffs, and holds its final brace until launch; cancellation restores the current behaviour pose. This is a physical wait, not a world freeze: moving support can carry the boss, support can be lost and hazards continue moving. After waiting, prove the takeoff again from its current position before jumping; an invalidated route does not launch. Escape cancels the pending takeoff. This preparation is separate from attack commitment and attack cooldown; ordinary pursuit walking continues without a takeoff warning
- **Idle recovery:** all six bosses share the watchdog `ANIMAL_IDLE_WATCHDOG` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`, which owns its stall time, minimum movement, search distance and attempt count. It arms only after actual acquisition and tracks horizontal physical displacement across graph changes and escape: vertical movement alone does not reset the clock. A stall without the minimum horizontal movement sends a ground boss to the movement map's nearest reachable position toward Chibi, or to a pace point when it is already there; Pebble instead searches within the search distance, checking a bounded number of candidate positions per attempt. It waits for a complete eligible physics tick rather than letting instant tell loops reset or starve that clock. Pebble makes independent seeded choices among safe lateral targets. Committed tells, recovery animations and airborne phases are protected. Recovery requires a verified supported ground position or perch and a safe swept route past hazards; if none is proved, the actor is not forced to move or teleported. Unknown geometry or exhausted work is not an unreachable verdict. Successful recovery resumes pursuit, and retry resets the watchdog. While engaged and more than two tiles from Chibi, a boss never goes more than four seconds without moving half a tile horizontally. An engaged ground boss holds still only during a tell, a ground takeoff preparation of at most 0.6 seconds (at most once per two seconds of chase), a chase pause of at most 1.4 seconds, a pace pause of at most 1.5 seconds or a post-attack rest: an authored committed rest for its own duration (Fert's 1 second throw pause), any other for at most 0.4 seconds
- **Keep clear:** after any ended chase, until its 5 second rearm wait ends, the boss never moves toward Chibi while he is within one screen width. Contact stays lethal. Pebble stays on or returns to his perch and never descends toward Chibi during the wait
- **Moves:** each chosen move holds for a seeded 0.6 to 0.9 seconds so Chibi can read its heading. Chase aims at Chibi along the movement map. Cut-off aims up to 8 tiles ahead of a running Chibi, with a 0.5 second lead, once he is at least 3 tiles clear and moving at least 8 tiles per second. Fert's standoff steps him back toward 4 tiles out when Chibi is within 2 to 4 tiles. Pebble's swoop setup flies 10 tiles farther along Chibi's travel while Chibi is in sight and the special is not due. When no map path reaches a move's target, the boss paces. Live collision, hazards, leash limits and attack checks remain authoritative. Bini faces the current target once when his maul tell begins
- **On screen:** every chosen wander walk, wander hop, Pebble relocation and walk home keeps the boss's body inside Chibi's screen: one screen width about his centre at decision time, inset 1 tile (`PURSUIT_LIMITS.screen` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`). Ground walks stop at the screen edge, and flights choose only landings inside it. A boss already outside his screen never moves farther from it, and walks home only while home lies nearer to him, including after a give-up or the 60-tile escape. An unengaged wander outside his screen is bounded only by its 4-tile range. A chosen destination and leg are kept even if Chibi moves afterwards, and nothing teleports
- **Scored decisions:** an engaged boss has one pursuit state; every finished attack returns to it, and there is no fixed attack rotation. Each decision scores the attacks whose gates hold (attack ready, range, sight, alignment) against the boss's moves, reading distance, horizontal and height gaps, line of sight, Chibi's speed and time since the last attack and special; an attack whose considerations hold outscores every move. Pressure starts 2 seconds after the last attack or chase pause and ramps over 6 seconds, raising attacks and closing moves; an attack also rises as its 20 second (any) or 40 second (special) cadence comes due, and an engagement opens with its special half due. The shared attack cooldown is the breather. Attacks are scored on the tick their gate opens, so a tell can start at once. Tells, committed attacks, airborne legs and takeoff preparation never decide. Weights and considerations are `src/data/bossScorers.ts`
- **Wander:** an unengaged boss is never parked. From its spawn, and after an ended chase's walk home and rearm wait, a ground boss starts a move every seeded 2 to 4 seconds: a walk of 1 to 2 tiles at half Chibi's walk to a seeded side along supported floor (the other side when that one leaves 4 tiles of its encounter position or Chibi's screen, or offers less than 1 tile); a walk that turns back is at least as long as the walk before it, stopping at walls and edges, then a hold of a seeded 1 to 2 seconds facing the walk direction in its idle pose. Pebble instead hops every seeded 3 to 5 seconds to a supported spot 2 to 4 tiles away and within 4 tiles of his perch, then perches for 1.5 to 3 seconds. An unengaged boss moves at least 1 tile horizontally within every 5 seconds. Spotting Chibi opens the boss's first move

## Boss encounter principles

Each boss encounter is a designed arena: a full chunk authored for its stage and boss, built from the stage's terrain and look, holding the stage's one encounter. Bosses are an annoyance that slows Chibi down, not a fight and not a killer: contact stays lethal, but every arena gives an ordinary way past. Apply these criteria to each arena, not only to the shared behaviour graph.

- **Arenas:** each encounter stage has exactly one arena (`src/data/arenas/`), admitted only once its zone's certified list names it. A fresh run places it at a seeded index in the second half of the stage's middle chunks, never the finish; retries keep it. An arena has at least two routes or levels and a loop of at least 20 tiles, so Chibi can always take another path, change level or jump away, and a refuge that counters its boss: high ground against charges (Archie, Trixi, Bini), cover against Fert's parcels and Pebble's droppings, and a low ceiling against Kiwi's climb and drop. Two arenas, City boundary and Ravine, also have a nook, a pocket 3 rows tall that only Chibi fits. There are no other boss-proof escape points. The boss starts between Chibi and the exit, so it slows him. Dash, Double jump and Speedy are never required. Pursuit ends beyond one screen width (60 tiles), at a room door or by giving up, and the stage start remains the only checkpoint
- **Evidence:** qualify the actual placement, body dimensions, terrain and current behaviour together. Observe the warning, committed motion, accepted effects, contact, physical recovery and next attack boundary. A state transition, observed spawn or successful planner query alone is not a full encounter pass. Unknown or exhausted planning work is not proof of an impossible route. Every told attack names at least one dodge in `src/data/bossFairness.ts`, each an automated script using ordinary movement only: jump-over (a full jump toward the far side within 4 tiles, from 0.45 seconds into the tell or at commitment), retreat (walk away, jumping when blocked) and under (walk toward and past the boss). Scripts start `reactionDelaySeconds` (0.25 seconds) after the tell starts. At every tell start in every boss scenario, at least one named dodge keeps Chibi alive through the attack's end plus the 0.4 second recovery bound, and across all scenarios every named dodge of an observed attack succeeds at least once. Human readability and enjoyment require playtesting. An arena is certified only when a skill-free evade controller, deciding from what Chibi sees 0.25 seconds late, passes 4 seeds from each of 2 starts: in all 8 runs Chibi reaches the exit alive within 60 seconds of the boss first acquiring him, with at least one route switch or loop level change, and in at least 6 of them he exits at least 2 seconds later than the paired run with the boss removed. The same slowdown threshold applies to every arena in all six zones
- **Flight:** provide overhead catch-up clearance and space for standoff swoops, avoiding stationary body blockades over the only route. Cover and landing supports must remain usable through the actual attack and return. Certify supported recovery before another attack; an airborne pause is not a perch or a completed landing
- **Identity:** give each boss one or two recognisable specials alongside ordinary pursuit and charges. Distinguish attacks through their actual movement, exposure, effects and ordinary responses, rather than only their names or animation tags
- **Readability:** retain the half-second to 1.2-second tell with its visible pose and silhouette outline before commitment. Place encounters so the cue and a useful ordinary response are observable before contact; a tell does not make an already overlapping body safe. Rendered phase, animation frame, effects and lethal contact must agree. Do not credit an unseen cue or obscure it with another presentation
- **Recovery:** preserve one committed attack at a time and the shared cooldown after physical completion. Test a useful forward getaway through that cycle. Cooldown permits pursuit and body contact remains lethal, so cooldown duration alone does not certify safe passage
- **Traversal and variety:** place bosses on real support, with body-sized clearance and physical routes across relevant blocks, ledges and gaps. Vary supported spawn positions and encounter geometry through seeded authored choices, while Retry preserves its layout and seed. More entries with equivalent geometry do not establish more distinct challenges. Test ordinary player traversal separately from boss traversal, including approaches and departures across chunk seams

## Forced transit

A forced-transit ride is a moving platform with an interval that offers no ordinary safe exit. Only explicitly authored, certified mandatory-ride metadata makes a ride forced; moving support, velocity, an endpoint or unknown geometry does not, and no per-tick planning infers it. A ride without a certified forced interval follows the ordinary attack rules.

- **Boarding:** only boarding from a bank the ride's metadata lists can start a protected leg; any other boarding follows the ordinary attack rules. Boarding starts unprotected, with attacks allowed, until the ride's forced window is certified. A certified forced window starts the protected leg
- **Deferral:** during a protected leg, Pebble starts no new targeted swoop or aimed poop burst. Every other attack and every untargeted hazard continues. Pebble may reposition safely overhead; an attack that becomes eligible again uses its normal full tell
- **Deferral end:** deferral ends at the first safe forward exit or useful refuge offered. Retreating alone does not end it. After an exit has been offered, staying on the ride does not rearm it
- **Disembark:** any safe grounded contact, authored bank or not, ends the protected leg, even while a ride still supports Chibi
- **Existing danger:** committed attacks, existing projectiles, body contact and red hazards remain lethal. Deferral is not invulnerability or a general moving-platform attack ban
- **Exit offer:** an exit is offered when an ordinary Jump, any release from tap to full hold, can begin on the next valid input, allowing one fixed-step release prefix while Jump is held, with actual carry, steering, coyote time and collision. Intentional grounded dwell, a run-up or waiting for a future dock is not an offered exit
- **New leg:** after safely getting off at a middle bank listed for a second ride, boarding any ride that lists that bank starts a fresh protected leg. Hopping directly between rides never re-arms protection. A hop from a still-deferring leg directly onto another ride keeps deferral until Chibi reaches safe ground; the second ride's exits never end the first leg
- **Unknown state:** while the ride state is unknown, attacks follow the last certified state. Unknown never certifies a forced window or proves an exit unavailable

## Archie introductions and encounters

- **Appearance:** Archie uses his design from Archie's Dog Walks
- **Cycle:** wander; on spotting Chibi, crouch, rush and rest before scored pursuit. Detection and pursuit use the shared enemy rules; the rush direction locks when crouching starts
- **Dialogue:** the first introduction has three lines: Archie treats Chibi as a parcel Fert asked him to stop; Chibi insists on walking home; Archie resolves to stop him. The player-facing wording lives in the text data files, with Skip available
- **Special:** Archie makes a leaping pounce as his distinct special attack, alongside periodic ground rushes for direct contact. Pounce preparation starts within its separately tuned engagement range; at the end of the full tell he rechecks that range and current line of sight. If either fails, he resumes pursuit without committing or starting attack cooldown

## Pebble encounter

- **Cycle:** wander by hopping between posts, spot Chibi and launch into flight. During pursuit, Pebble ascends above Chibi before flying ahead along Chibi's captured travel direction, using his facing when stationary. Terrain or hazards that block this overhead route do not permit a ground-height chase or a falsely reached target. The scorer picks his drop approach (8 tiles ahead) or swoop setup (18 tiles ahead), with no fixed rotation; reaching that goal may start a fresh tell; preparation tracks live Chibi, and commitment still checks live alignment, line of sight, the minimum swoop gap and the full tell, `SPECIAL_TELL_SECONDS` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`. An approach that opens no attack relocates after 1.5 seconds, or 1 second with Chibi out of sight. Relocation chooses a seeded supported destination within the horizontal distance band and inside Chibi's screen at decision time, checks up to 8 candidates and proves an ascent, overhead cruise and descent with overhead vertical clearance; `PIGEON_RELOCATION_SELECTION` in the same file owns these values. Pebble does not slide along the ground or reroll his destination during the leg. After landing he may resume pursuit when attack cooldown and line of sight permit; otherwise he waits up to the relocation wait, `PIGEON_RELOCATION_SECONDS` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`, before relocating again. A failed or unknown uncommitted relocation returns to the ordinary nearest supported landing and the relocation wait, without a warning or attack cooldown. An uncommitted drop or swoop tell lasts at most `SPECIAL_TELL_SECONDS`. If its commitment checks still fail at that deadline, Pebble cancels the tell, clears the warning and returns to actual support without starting attack cooldown. A blocked or invalid overhead goal cancels the uncommitted approach or tell and returns him to actual support and rest before a fresh attempt, without declaring Chibi permanently unreachable. Swoops require physical horizontal separation before both preparation and commitment; if Chibi gets too close, Pebble cancels the uncommitted tell, repositions safely and starts a fresh full tell without starting attack cooldown. The swoop side follows the approach direction rather than a rigid alternating side. Pebble periodically makes committed contact dives with a distinct preparation, slower than his cruise, and separately attacks exposed Chibi with three-shot white-block poop bursts. Before each drop he aligns with Chibi's current horizontal position using a small bounded lead and fresh line of sight; a drop tell commits within 0.5 tiles of its overhead goal (`PIGEON_DROP_COMMIT_TOLERANCE` in `src/data/behaviours.ts`), so a started drop lands on a moving Chibi. Solid cover blocks the lane; blocked or stale alignment returns to pursuit instead of forcing a drop. Each completed burst or dive returns to actual safe supported ground or a perch through the same two-step landing as an abandoned attack and rests there before the next attack. Ordinary support contact counts: part of his body on the support is enough. Support loss resumes physical landing and restarts the supported dwell. The attack remains committed until that dwell finishes, then its cooldown begins. Without a safe landing route, recovery stays pending without another attack, false completion or teleportation. Acquired pursuit and committed recovery may cross chunk seams while checking actual area collisions and hazards. Flight, dive and projectile tuning live in shared behaviour data
- **Layout:** Freight terminal's arena combines solid overhead cover with exposed flight and projectile lanes
- **Projectiles:** each white block falls straight down and is lethal on contact. It disappears on landing, including on a ledge, leaving no residue and never homing on Chibi

## Trixi encounter

- **Appearance:** Trixi wears round glasses: in profile, one round lens over a visible eye
- **Rush:** Trixi periodically rushes along the ground to make contact with Chibi. A committed rush, however it ends, overshoots by a 3-tile slide, leaving space behind her; the slide ends early at a wall or ledge
- **Traps:** Trixi's special attack places red-edged floor traps. A visible arming delay precedes their lethal contact state; trap timing and placement values live in data

## Bini encounter

- **Cycle:** wander, open with the sonic growl special attack, then choose among growl, charge and maul by score: the growl only near his height, the maul only close and the charge only level. Timing and movement values live in data
- **Growl:** the sonic growl creates a brief gust that pushes Chibi only while he overlaps its finite volume. The push stops when he is above or outside it, or behind solid cover; it is not a single impulse that leaves momentum. It does not kill directly, but can push him into a hazard or off a ledge. High ground is the primary way to avoid it; solid cover also blocks it
- **Layout:** Bini's arena gives accessible high ground and cover from the growl, alongside exposed lanes where a connected growl can push Chibi off a ledge
- **Introduction:** Bini's portrait holds a phone playing Archie's Dog Walks; his annoyed lines open the Forest paths introduction, which follows the once-per-zone rule and precedes his aggressive behaviour

## Kiwi encounter

- **Cycle:** Kiwi periodically charges twice along the ground for direct contact; the second charge follows its own re-aimed tell with the full outline; she skips the second charge when less than 3 tiles of ground lie ahead toward Chibi. Her distinct special attack climbs, prepares and drops vertically before resting; she climbs only for this attack, and her pursuit routes stay on the ground. A return stuck for 3.2 seconds completes into pursuit. Ground movement, vertical movement and state timings live in shared behaviour data
- **Layout:** Familiar neighbourhood's arena has low ceilings she cannot climb past, so standing beneath one blocks her climb-and-drop. Before climbing she lines up horizontally with Chibi, so a low ceiling over him blocks the drop

## Fert encounter

On first spotting Chibi, Fert opens with a parcel tell and throw, then his throw pause, instead of a charge. He periodically charges and throws parcels through shared behaviour primitives and is always lethal on contact. After each parcel throw he stands still for 1 second as part of the committed throw; the attack cooldown begins after this pause
