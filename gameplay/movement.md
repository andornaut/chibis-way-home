# Movement and skills

Chibi’s ordinary movement, grid reaches and optional skills. [Stage principles](stages.md#stage-design-principles) apply these reaches to authored routes.

## Grid

- **Chibi:** 2 tiles wide and 3 tall
- **Reaches:** stages are built to Chibi's reaches, measured in tiles from the top of the ground he leaves to the top of the ledge he lands on. A short press rises 3 tiles (clearing a 3-tile ledge with a little room); a full jump rises 6; an ordinary full running jump with flutter crosses a 17-tile gap at the same height, with the jump pressed at its best moment, coyote time included. A ledge at a reach is always makeable, and one tile beyond it never is
- **Tiles:** stages are built on a grid of 12 px tiles, so the screen is 60 columns by 30 rows. Every solid, ledge and door sits on whole tiles and spans whole tiles. Hazards follow the grid, except for the thickness of edge hazards
- **Travel distances:** generally author movement travel distances as whole multiples of the tile size defined in `src/data/screen.ts`, including dash and moving-object routes. Physics remains smooth and continuous: this does not quantize per-step displacement, speed, acceleration or gravity, or snap final positions to the grid. Collisions, support and endpoint constraints can stop movement between grid lines

## Chibi

- **Abilities:** new games start with no owned skills. Entering a gift room permanently grants Dash, Double jump or Speedy. Ownership and active selection are separate: at most one skill is selected, and only its effect applies. No grant selects its skill, the first included: every grant keeps the current selection, so a new game plays with no skill until the player selects one. Each newly owned skill triggers its power-up tip, and owning a second skill triggers the switch tip. See [player copy](player-copy.md#dialogue) for tip timing. An already owned grant triggers neither tip. Revisiting an already owned grant changes nothing and does not stack it. Ordinary required routes are completable without any skill, while skills can help with optional shortcuts, boss avoidance, secrets and dialogue access. There is no life counter or extra-life mechanic; later progression is listed in [`next.md`](next.md)
- **Cheats:** see [Cheats](cheats.md) for entry, saving and controls. No cheat selects a power-up
- **Double jump:** with Double jump selected, after leaving support, one fresh airborne Jump press can start an additional jump with half the first jump's maximum rise. Its short-hop rise scales by the same ratio, with the same release and hold controls. A grounded or coyote-time jump does not consume that extra jump. Actual supported landing restores it; holding Jump does not repeatedly trigger it
- **Flutter:** every fall flutters, after a jump or off a ledge: his fall is slow enough to use, and the player steers him in the air
- **Jump height:** every jump has one uninterrupted decelerating ascent, with no timed lift, upward reboost or pause at the apex while Jump is held. Releases within the tap window produce the minimum hop, which clears the short-press reach under Grid; releasing later blends continuously toward a higher apex, up to the full height, without a velocity snap, upward reboost or apex pause. A buffered jump whose button was already released is the low hop. Low ceilings are placed so a short jump clears the obstacle without touching the overhead red edge; a full hold hits that edge. Jump tuning, including the tap window and hop height, lives in `src/data/bird.ts`
- **Jump timing:** a jump pressed just after he leaves a ledge still jumps (coyote time), and a jump pressed just before he lands jumps as he lands (jump buffering)
- **Movement:** he walks left and right and jumps. He accelerates and brakes smoothly on the ground and in the air. His speeds, acceleration, braking, jump, flutter and timing values are in `src/data/bird.ts`, tuned to the reaches under Grid
- **Skill controls:** eligible skills appear on the gameplay HUD. They stay visible throughout dialogue: barks, conversation lines, required choices and departures. A physical modal card, including Pause, dims and disables covered HUD controls and the shared entry while leaving them painted. Owned or temporarily eligible skills stay visible; selection and resource state determine their appearance. During uncovered active gameplay, tapping an eligible unselected icon selects it and tapping the selected icon turns it off; holding for the duration in `SKILL_GESTURE` in `src/data/skills.ts` opens details and pauses play. Skill controls take no presses under Pause or another physical modal. Closing details resumes gameplay only when no other input owner holds it
- **Skill states:** glyph and disc sizes and hit geometry stay fixed across enabled normal, cooldown, disabled and unselected states; enabling a skill never enlarges it. In the normal palette, the selected skill has a yellow face and ready glyph when available, or a dark slate face and muted glyph on cooldown. A skill superseded by another selection is disabled and shows a navy face with a muted glyph. With no selection, all eligible skills use that same navy and muted appearance. See the [button-state rules](rules.md#interface) for input cues and forced colours. Selected Double jump stays normal while its extra jump is unspent, including on the ground, during coyote time and while Jump is held; spending it mutes the icon until actual support resets it. Selected Dash is muted during its active burst, actual cooldown or a spent airborne charge; being grounded or holding an input alone does not mute it. Selected Speedy stays normal as a passive effect
- **Skills:** ownership and the active selection persist across retries, stage changes and reopening, and reset on confirmed Restart. Capability fields in `src/data/bird.ts` describe mechanics, not saved ownership. Unlock all power-ups in Cheats saves ownership of every power-up and leaves the current selection as it was. Selecting an owned skill persists normally, with or without cheats. Preview-specific save guards are unchanged
- **Speedy:** selecting Speedy increases normal horizontal running and airborne control speed by 20%, using `SKILLS.speedyWalkMultiplier` in `src/data/skills.ts`, without changing dash speed, jump height or acceleration. Repeated grants do not multiply it again
