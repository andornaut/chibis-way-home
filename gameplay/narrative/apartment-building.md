# Apartment building

## Established direction

Fert, a theatrical ferret, is Chibi's former best friend and apartment-mate. Chibi returns to reclaim his home. Their fight's cause and the friendship's outcome remain undecided. The building has six floor stages in this order: Lobby, Restaurant, Pool, Spa, Residential floors and Penthouse.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Make familiar domestic details the payoff for the long journey. Fert's confidence in distance has failed; his remaining obstruction is personal proximity. Chibi's claim can become more direct without forcing an apology, forgiveness or a final fight.

Give the floors different narrative functions within one ascent:

- **Lobby:** the building is recognizable, but reaching it is not yet reaching the apartment.
- **Restaurant:** ordinary activity continues around the disagreement; Chibi is returning to a lived-in place.
- **Pool:** reflections and open views briefly reveal the remaining ascent.
- **Spa:** a quieter interior makes Fert's theatrical obstruction seem increasingly out of place.
- **Residential floors:** repeated everyday details become specific to Chibi's home.
- **Penthouse:** arrive at the shared apartment and establish Chibi's right to be there, leaving the relationship unresolved.

**Incoming beat:** Chibi looks from the pavement towards his apartment window, then enters the Lobby. The ascent connects the external journey to a familiar interior.

**Outgoing beat proposal:** hold on a shared domestic detail after Chibi returns, without deciding whether Fert stays, leaves or reconciles. The precise final image requires a separate story choice.

Stairs and elevators belong to the main ascent, not an item-lock puzzle. Friendly rooms are optional side visits.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Purl, a hedgehog building resident, in a mending room beside the Residential floors.

**Item proposal:** a dropped spool visible on an intermediate landing. A short vertical detour alternates an interior platform with an exterior refuge, then reconnects beside Purl's room through a clearly visible shortcut. It must not require revisiting several completed floors or obtaining access to another resident's home.

**Reward proposal:** a small repaired decoration for Chibi's travel card. Prefer a cosmetic here so an untested buff does not determine the final encounter. Purl does not adjudicate the fight or provide a required key to Chibi's apartment.
