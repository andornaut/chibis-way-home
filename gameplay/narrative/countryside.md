# Countryside

## Established foundation

Farmland, Country lanes and River crossing use yellow countryside scenery, soil, hay, banks and timber. Trixi is a goose with oversized glasses who invents convenient rules while helping Fert. Decorative water is not an implicit lethal surface.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Open views make progress towards home visible, while fences and riverbanks complicate the direct line. Trixi presents her personal obstruction as a regulation; Chibi need not win an argument to continue. The absurd rules can be funny without making the friendship dispute trivial.

**Incoming beat:** a straight freight line becomes a winding farm track. A distant city silhouette offers the first recognizable direction home.

**Outgoing beat:** the far bank narrows into a tree-lined path; the skyline disappears behind leaves, but Chibi keeps moving towards it.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Clover, a rabbit gardener, in a potting shed.

**Item proposal:** a seed packet visible on a high hay shelf. Take a short rising route, time a moving ledge over a shallow scenic channel, and return along a lower fence-side shortcut. Keep the safe bank and any actual red surfaces visually distinct.

**Reward proposal:** a flower pin or a sketch identifying a safe bank route. Returning the packet is an optional favour, not a payment needed to cross the river. No crop-growing timer, water immunity or extra life is implied.
