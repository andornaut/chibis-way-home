# Blue cityscape

## Established foundation

Parcel depot, Streets and City boundary introduce Chibi's homeward journey. Archie is an earnest territorial dog, stopping Chibi as a favour to Fert. The opened parcel anchors the beginning; movement and timing, not combat, carry Chibi through the city.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Treat the depot as an organized place with one conspicuous administrative problem: its parcel has walked away. Chibi initially has only the certainty that Fert sent him here. Archie focuses on stopping the delivery rather than judging the fight.

**Incoming beat:** Chibi pushes aside the parcel flap; the address and unfamiliar surrounding streets establish the distance without explaining the quarrel. Keep any readable address fictional and subordinate to the visual.

**Outgoing beat:** the buildings thin out, and a freight gantry appears beyond the city boundary. Chibi follows the transport corridor in the opposite spirit to the parcel journey: this time he chooses the direction.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Mallow, a mouse parcel clerk, in a sorting office. Mallow recognizes an odd delivery but neither knows the fight's cause nor speaks for Fert.

**Item proposal:** a loose sorting stamp, visible on an upper shelf from the primary route. Reach it through a short low-ceiling hop and an upper ledge, then drop through a clearly visible return opening beside the office. This teaches a different use of familiar movement without hiding the stage exit.

**Reward proposal:** Mallow marks a simple sketch showing an upcoming upper refuge, or gives a decorative stamp for a travel card. Neither is required for progression. This is the recommended first optional-content prototype; persistence and repeat rewards remain undecided.
