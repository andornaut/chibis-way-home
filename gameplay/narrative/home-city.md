# Home city

## Established foundation

City outskirts, Back streets and Familiar neighbourhood lead towards Chibi's apartment. Kiwi is a dry, observant female raccoon who watches from high places and obstructs Chibi as a favour to Fert. Climbing, cover and vertical attack lanes distinguish her encounters.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Let recognition, rather than increased exposition, carry the story. A familiar shopfront, courtyard and apartment silhouette progressively replace generic city landmarks. Kiwi notices Chibi's shortcuts; Chibi knows why he is taking them. Neither character needs to establish who was right in the original fight.

**Incoming beat:** the ravine view becomes a street-level view of the same roofline. Chibi recognizes the city before reaching his own neighbourhood.

**Outgoing beat:** frame the apartment building from the pavement, then cut to its lobby entrance. Home is now a specific place rather than a distant direction.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Juniper, a cat who repairs umbrellas, in a small neighbourhood shop. Any prior acquaintance with Chibi is also a proposal, not established history.

**Item proposal:** a ribbon caught on a balcony rail. Climb a side stair, observe Kiwi's vertical lane from solid cover, and cross onto a separate balcony. A short drop returns to the shop without re-entering the attack lane.

**Reward proposal:** a decorative ribbon tied to Chibi's travel card, or a hint identifying a sheltered apartment approach. The ribbon is not a home key and does not unlock the building. The optional exchange can recognize Chibi's return without resolving his relationship with Fert.
