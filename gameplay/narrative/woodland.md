# Woodland

## Established foundation

Forest paths, Deep woods and Ravine bring roots, trees and stone into the route. Bini is a bear distracted by his phone game, Archie's Dog Walks. He obstructs Chibi for Fert; his growl makes accessible high ground and solid cover important.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Make this the journey's most enclosed stretch. Nearby branches and rock obscure the distant destination, so Chibi follows the route rather than a constant skyline. Bini regards the interruption as an inconvenience, not evidence that Chibi deserves exile. His game reference can remain a brief joke rather than a separate subplot.

**Incoming beat:** foliage closes around the countryside path. The bright phone screen establishes Bini before a wider clearing reveals the route past him.

**Outgoing beat:** the ravine opens onto a view of the home city. Familiar roof shapes replace the anonymous skyline and confirm that the long crossing has mattered.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Moss, a badger mapmaker, in a trail shelter.

**Item proposal:** a trail marker caught on a root shelf. Reach it by a deliberate climb around a red-edged obstacle, use a high refuge to observe the return path, then descend onto a shelter-side shortcut. The detour must not demand crossing Bini's pressure lane twice.

**Reward proposal:** a route drawing that calls attention to an upcoming high refuge. This reinforces observation rather than granting growl immunity. Retrieving the marker never repairs the only bridge or becomes a required navigation key.
