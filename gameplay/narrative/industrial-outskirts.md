# Industrial outskirts

## Established foundation

Warehouse district, Loading yards and Freight terminal use crates, containers and moving platforms. Pebble is a smug pigeon who claims the airspace while doing Fert a favour. His flight and attacks make overhead shelter relevant.

## Proposed dramatic and visual direction

Contrast a place built to move cargo with Chibi's determination to move himself. Pebble mistakes access to the sky for control of every route. Chibi's answer is practical: surfaces and shelter still provide a way through. Fert's attempt to remove him remains the motive, not a newly revealed conspiracy.

**Incoming beat:** the last city street lines up with warehouse loading lanes; a passing container briefly hides Chibi before he emerges on the far side.

**Outgoing beat:** gantries recede behind an open horizon. The freight corridor gives way to fields rather than becoming a vehicle Chibi must unlock.

## Proposed optional room and detour

**Friendly character proposal:** Rivet, a mole maintenance worker, in a quiet service workshop.

**Item proposal:** a misplaced winding handle on a loading platform. Observe a shuttle, board it, cross beneath a solid shelter and transfer to a side shelf. A descending return ledge leads to the workshop without retracing exposed airspace.

**Reward proposal:** Rivet points out the sheltered side of a later crossing. A one-hit shield could be an alternative prototype reward under the shared proposal, but is not part of this design's assumed rules. The handle does not repair required machinery or gate the stage.
