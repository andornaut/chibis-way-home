Controls

Gameplay
Action Input
Jump Space, W, K or Up arrow; left mouse button; touch Jump. Hold for a higher jump.
Move A, H or Left arrow; D, L or Right arrow. On touch, drag left or right off a button.
Pause P, or tap or click Pause.
Use E, F or Enter; right mouse button; touch Use.
Menu
Action Input
Activate E, F, Enter or Space on a focused action; tap, click or right-click an available button.
Back or skip Escape uses visible Skip first, then an allowed Close or Back. Required choices stay open.
Focus Arrow keys, W/A/S/D or K/H/J/L; Tab forward or Shift+Tab back.

On the map, Left/A/H and Right/D/L choose a destination. Use Continue to select a stage. Native text fields and sliders keep their own keys.
